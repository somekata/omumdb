Outreach

Growing the community of people who enjoy bacteriology

We use characters and games to make bacteria and infectious diseases more familiar and more fun.

Baikinz®

Baikinz® is a set of original characters based on microorganisms (a registered trademark). They also appear in our monthly calendar.

FunFection

FunFection is our brand for educational games and tools, with the tagline “Fun is contagious.” Using the Baikinz® characters, we are developing games such as:

  • Baikinz Tōmetsu Senki: Busters Schule — a turn-based RPG built around the WHO AWaRe classification
  • BactAttack — a shooting game built around the WHO AWaRe classification (with an English version)

AMR awareness

We received the Minister of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology Award for our work on raising awareness of antimicrobial resistance (AMR).
