Research themes

Antimicrobial resistance (AMR)

Research on difficult-to-treat resistant bacteria such as Pseudomonas aeruginosa and Acinetobacter baumannii.

Fungi and antifungal agents

Analysis of infections caused by Candida species, and the search for novel antifungal compounds from deep-sea actinomycetes.

Clinical bacteriology and education

Developing gamification-based methods for teaching bacteriology and infectious diseases.

Biosafety and collaboration

Activities of the BSL committee and collaborative frameworks for infectious disease control, such as OIRCID.

Education and outreach

ILOHA

An infectious disease study group running since 2014, with more than 430 sessions held.

bactPlatform

A library of HTML-based tools for learning microbiology and infectious diseases.

Baikinz® and FunFection

"Fun is infectious." Bringing bacteriology closer through games and characters.

Baikinz® Calendar

A monthly calendar, updated every month. November 2026: "Eleven Microbe Samurai."
