Research themes

Antimicrobial resistance (AMR)

Resistant pathogens that are difficult to treat, such as Pseudomonas aeruginosa and Acinetobacter baumannii.

Fungi and antifungal discovery

Infections caused by Candida species, and the search for novel antifungal compounds from deep-sea actinomycetes.

Clinical bacteriology and education

Gamification-based methods for teaching bacteriology and infectious diseases.

Biosafety and collaboration

The BSL committee and collaborative frameworks such as OIRCID.

Education and outreach

ILOHA

An infectious disease study group running since 2014, with more than 430 sessions held.

bactPlatform

A library of HTML learning tools for microbiology and infectious diseases.

Baikinz® and FunFection

“Fun is contagious.” Games and characters that bring bacteriology closer to everyone.
