Education

Our approach

In teaching bacteriology and infectious diseases, we value enjoying the subject as much as knowing it. Alongside undergraduate and graduate teaching, we use gamification and self-made learning tools to increase the number of people who enjoy bacteriology.

ILOHA

ILOHA (Infection Lecture at OMU Hospital & Affiliates) is an infectious disease study group that has been running since 2014, with more than 430 sessions held. Announcements are sent through an official LINE account.

bactPlatform

A library of HTML learning tools for microbiology and infectious diseases, published on GitHub Pages.

  • Microbiology laboratory simulators
  • Immunology and parasitology series
  • Clinical history-taking simulators
  • Infectious disease chart viewers
