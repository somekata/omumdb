Research

Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) and resistant bacteria

AMR is a serious global problem. We study the epidemiology of resistance and infection control, mainly for multidrug-resistant Gram-negative bacilli that are difficult to treat, such as Pseudomonas aeruginosa and Acinetobacter baumannii.

  • Analysis of P. aeruginosa showing difficult-to-treat resistance (DTR)
  • Meta-analysis of novel antimicrobial agents for carbapenem-resistant A. baumannii (CRAB)
  • Infection control for multidrug-resistant Gram-negative bacilli

Fungi (Candida species)

We integrate clinical data on invasive infections caused by Candida species, including systematic reviews and meta-analyses that compare mortality in candidemia caused by Candida auris and by other Candida species.

Novel antifungal compounds from deep-sea actinomycetes

We study actinomycetes isolated from the deep sea as a source of novel antifungal compounds. Genome analysis with a nanopore sequencer (MinION), compound analysis by HPLC/MS, RNA-seq, and evaluation in mice are combined to search for and evaluate candidate compounds.

Clinical bacteriology and education research

We study how to teach clinical microbiology so that the knowledge can be used in practice. We develop gamified learning materials, including games built around the WHO AWaRe classification of antibacterial agents. See Education and Outreach for details.

Biosafety

Through the Biosafety Level (BSL) committee of Osaka Metropolitan University, we contribute to building systems for handling pathogens safely.

Collaboration on infectious disease control (OIRCID)

OIRCID (Osaka International Research Center for Infectious Diseases) is a five-party framework of Osaka Prefecture, Osaka City, Osaka Metropolitan University, Osaka University, and the Osaka Institute of Public Health. Our department is also involved in designing its governance.
