About

About the department

The Department of Bacteriology at Osaka Metropolitan University Graduate School of Medicine works on bacterial and fungal infections and antimicrobial resistance (AMR), education in clinical microbiology, and outreach that makes bacteriology and infectious diseases more approachable.

Our aim: growing the community of people who enjoy bacteriology

We want to turn bacteriology from “difficult” into “fun.” Through games and learning tools, our education and outreach aim to increase the number of people who enjoy bacteria (“saikin-raku jinkō” in Japanese).
